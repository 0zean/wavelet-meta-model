Meta-Labeling Zoo Study

We searched primary signals, meta-models, feature sets, bet sizers and retraining cadences across 19 US symbols and five bar sizes, counting every fitted configuration as a trial. Fourteen finalists were then run once on a year of data nobody had looked at.

Verdict: no robust out-of-sample edge. Thirteen finalists are not demonstrated and one is negative. Their holdout Sharpes average 0.02 with a spread of 1.03, which is what fourteen strategies with no edge would produce over 248 days. Development Sharpe did not predict holdout Sharpe (rank correlation −0.15).

Stage funnel

Each stage kept fewer cells, and the edge thinned with them

Every selection rule was written into PLAN.md before the stage's results were read. Survivors are cells that passed the stage's gates; trials are the counted fits added by the stage (a walk-forward cell counts every combo on its grid).

Gates in B1–B3 and C: probabilistic Sharpe PSR(0) > 0.5, beta-hedged alpha Sharpe > 0, best five days < 100 % of profit. Stage D (portfolio) was not run because no finalist passed Stage C.

Stage A · screen

Where the screen found something

475 cells: 19 symbols × 5 bar sizes × 5 primary signals, each with an xgboost meta-model, fixed sizing and bi-weekly retraining. A cell passed with out-of-sample meta AUC > 0.515 and PSR > 0.5. Each square counts how many of the five primaries passed.

Reading the screen

  • 54 of 475 cells passed (11 %). By bar size: 5Min 3, 15Min 12, 30Min 10, 1Hour 13, 1Day 16.
  • By primary: Bollinger mean reversion 15, Donchian breakout 14, wavelet trend 13, ML xgboost 9, SMA cross 3.
  • QQQ, XLK and AMZN account for 29 of the 54. IWM, META and TLT passed nothing.
  • PBO across the stage's 548 distinct return streams is 0.51: picking the best Stage A cell is a coin flip out of sample.
Stage B · refine

Models, features and sizing changed little

Each sub-stage changed one axis on the previous survivors and kept a change only when the pre-registered rule chose it.

B1 · meta-model

Chosen by highest out-of-sample AUC; 23 of 54 passed. Fresh models regressed (median Sharpe 0.11–0.18 against the selected xgboost rows' 0.23).

B2 · feature set

More features and clustered MDA selection lowered AUC against the default set; 22 of 23 passed.

B3 · bet sizer

Sizers mostly rescaled risk. All 22 passed; the top two per symbol by PSR (14) became finalists.

Risk profiles were not ablated: every U11 cell ran with risk profile none, and the portfolio stage that would have tested them (D) was not reached.

Stage C · power walk-forward

Rolling retraining erased most of the Stage B edge

Each finalist was re-run as a rolling walk-forward over a grid of in-sample lengths (IS) and retraining cadences (OOS), with the combo chosen walk-forward from trailing results. Squares show the median development Sharpe of each combo across the finalists; the small figure is the median walk-forward efficiency (WFE, out-of-sample return over in-sample return).

Intraday finalists (9) · IS × OOS in sessions

Daily finalists (5)

Intraday median WFE of −0.02 to 0.06 means out-of-sample returns were at most 6 % of what the same models earned on their own fitting data. At 1Day the IS grid starts at 1,260 sessions because shorter windows hold too few labeled events to fit.

Rule to reach Stage D: the walk-forward stream passes the B gates and PBO across the cell's combos is below 0.5. None passed. The five cells that kept a positive stream were positive in nearly every combo, but their PBO of 0.62–0.82 says choosing among those combos was itself overfit.

Retraining cadence and IS length

No cadence is recommended, because no finalist has a demonstrated edge to retrain. Descriptively, the longest intraday look-back was the most consistent: IS 756 sessions, retrained every 10 sessions had median development Sharpe 0.22 across the 9 intraday finalists (7 positive), median WFE 0.047, over 169 out-of-sample windows. For daily bars, IS 1,512 / OOS 10 had median Sharpe 0.70, WFE 0.33, over 93 windows. Retraining cadence (5 to 63 sessions) made no consistent difference.

Overfitting by stage

Probability of backtest overfitting

PBO (combinatorially symmetric cross-validation) estimates how often the cell that looks best in-sample lands below the median out of sample, over the days every cell in the stage covers.

Method

How the study was kept honest, and what it cannot say

Safeguards

  • Every fitted configuration is a ledger trial; the holdout DSR uses all 1,402.
  • Each selection rule was committed before its stage's results were read. Three amendments were made and recorded, each before the affected results existed: Stage A kept all 54 gate-passers (DSR ranking was degenerate), Stage C used a per-timeframe grid (the default grid could not fit any daily window), and Stage E tests with Holm across the 14 rather than full-search DSR (the holdout carries no selection bias).
  • The holdout was opened once, as batch 33b8b08e. Three worker crashes during it (memory corruption inside forest code) were re-run within the same batch, and every re-run cell reproduced its development stream exactly.

Limits

  • One year of holdout has little power: a strategy with a true Sharpe of 1.4 clears the first Holm step about 10 % of the time. Not demonstrated means the holdout could not confirm an edge.
  • The universe is 19 large, currently listed symbols, so delisted names are missing (survivorship bias).
  • Daily finalists have short walk-forward streams (752 development days) because their windows need 5 to 6 years of history.
  • Risk overlays and the portfolio stage were never reached.
